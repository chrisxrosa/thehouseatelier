Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Are you a property manager?

No. We handle the commercial side: pricing, presentation and channels. Cleaning, keys and guests stay with you or whoever helps you now.

Who owns my listings?

You do. Your listings, booking channels and accounts stay in your name. In some cases we connect them to our own tools to do the work, but they always remain yours.

What if my listing already performs well?

Then the Revenue Check will confirm it, and usually show where there's still room. You decide from there.

Can I book just photos?

Yes. In the form, pick "Photography" under "I'd like". Curious what your listing could earn too? Add the free Revenue Check.
