Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Are you a property manager?

No. We handle the commercial side: pricing, presentation and channels. Cleaning, keys and guests stay with you or whoever helps you now.

Who owns my accounts?

You do. Airbnb, Booking.com, PriceLabs and OwnerRez stay in your name. We work inside them as team members.

What software do I pay for?

PriceLabs for dynamic pricing, roughly $10 to $20 a month. With extra channels or a direct site, OwnerRez too. Exact costs are in your strategy.

What if my listing already performs well?

Then the strategy will show it, and usually where there's still room. You decide from there.

Can I book just photos?

Yes. Choose "A photo shoot" in the form. We include a quick revenue snapshot on shoot day.

What about the proposed 2027 tourist tax?

The draft budget proposes replacing the 7% accommodation tax with a per-visitor entry fee. It isn't law yet. If it passes, we'll adjust your pricing for it.
