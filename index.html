Before you apply

Frequently asked.

Is this only for brand-new listings?

The inaugural launch offer is designed primarily for new listings, particularly properties with little or no review history. Existing listings can also be a fit when the property has outgrown its current presentation or needs a visual reset.

What happens after I apply?

We review the property, confirm fit and availability, and then schedule the work. A deposit is collected to secure the shoot; the remaining balance is due on delivery.

How long is the shoot?

Most 2–3 bedroom properties take roughly 75–90 minutes. Larger or more complex properties receive additional time based on scope.

Can you implement the recommendations too?

Yes. Implementation and ongoing STR optimization are available separately for owners who want the strategy handled for them.
