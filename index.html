Before you apply

Frequently asked.

Is this only for brand-new listings?

The inaugural launch offer is designed primarily for new listings, particularly properties with little or no review history. Existing listings can also be a strong fit when the property has outgrown its current presentation or needs a visual and positioning reset.

What happens after I submit the form?

We review the property, confirm fit and availability, and then schedule the work. A deposit is collected for the work to be rendered, with the remaining balance due on delivery. There is no commitment until we confirm the property is a match.

How long does the work take on site?

It depends on the size and complexity of the property. Most projects take between 1 and 4 hours on site, with larger or more detailed properties requiring more time.

Do I have to hand over management of my property?

No. We build the foundation while you remain in the driver’s seat. You keep control of the property, the brand, the positioning, and the business. Implementation and ongoing STR optimization are available only if and when you want additional support.

Can you support the property beyond the initial work?

Yes. We can continue supporting the property with implementation and ongoing STR optimization. Services can include listing updates, direct booking marketing, a dedicated website, video walkthroughs for asynchronous guest communication, ongoing pricing support, and other property-specific needs.

Does this set up dynamic pricing?

The inaugural package establishes the pricing structure and competitive logic needed to move toward dynamic rate management. Ongoing revenue management and platform setup can be added separately when you are ready.
