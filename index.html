Frequently asked questions

Before you apply.

Is this only for brand-new listings?

New listings are the main fit, especially with little or no review history. Existing listings can fit when the presentation or positioning needs a reset.

What happens after I submit the form?

We review the property, confirm fit and availability, then call you to arrange the work. A deposit starts the project, and the balance is due on delivery.

How long does the work take on site?

Most projects take 1–4 hours on site. Larger or more detailed properties may take longer.

Do I have to hand over management of my property?

No. You keep the property, brand, positioning, and business. Ongoing optimization is optional.

Can you support the property beyond the initial work?

Yes. Optional support can include listing updates, direct-booking marketing, a website, video walkthroughs, and ongoing pricing.
